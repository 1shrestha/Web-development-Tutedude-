About Bella Vista

Our Story

Bella Vista" (Italian/Spanish for "beautiful view") refers to several distinct locations and properties: a scenic city in Arkansas, a historic Philadelphia neighborhood, a luxurious resort in Mahabaleshwar, a boutique hotel in Panchkula, and a palace in Hyderabad

Key Locations and Properties: Bella Vista, Arkansas (USA): A city in Northwest Arkansas known for its seven lakes, seven golf courses, and extensive mountain biking trails. Bella Vista, Philadelphia (USA): A historic neighborhood in South Philadelphia, known for its Italian heritage and the Italian Market. Bella Vista Resort, Mahabaleshwar (India): A popular pet-free resort and spa in Maharashtra featuring panoramic views, a "Red Stone Wing" and proximity to nature. Welcomhotel by ITC Hotels, Bella Vista (Panchkula, India): A boutique luxury hotel in the foothills of the Himalayas, offering 49 rooms, a rooftop pool, and close proximity to Chandigarh.
